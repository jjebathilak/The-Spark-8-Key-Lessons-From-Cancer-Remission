# The Spark ✨

*Eight small gems from sixteen months I never planned for.*

## About

In August 2024, at the busiest point of my life, I was diagnosed with cancer. Now in remission, I've written down the eight "gems" that season taught me — honest lessons on comparison, humility, emotions, finding fun in hardship, and what remains when your safety nets fall away.

This began as a farewell note to my colleagues, written for anyone who feels their spark fading.

## The Eight Gems

1. **The game of comparison** — the true gems live in the grey areas
2. **Whose life is harder?** — holding victories with gratitude, not ownership
3. **Remove the ceiling** — don't shrink your hopes to a "reasonable" size
4. **Keeping the spark lit** — bravery, and creating new memories to slow time down
5. **The sweetness of your emotions** — feeling nothing is not the same as being strong
6. **You can have fun at all times** — there is beauty in every situation
7. **Change wrestling partners** — knowing when to reassess what you're chasing
8. **Rock bottom** — discovering what's permanent when the safety nets fall away
